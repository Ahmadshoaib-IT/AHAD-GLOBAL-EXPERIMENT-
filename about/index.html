About

Most institutional failure is a failure of systems, not intent.

We work at the join between the plan and the machinery meant to carry it.

Who we are

Strategies are sound, mandates are clear, funding is secured, and delivery still breaks at the join between the plan and the machinery meant to carry it.

We work at that join. Across Management Consultancy and Digital Transformation Architecture, we convert ideas, opportunities and reform agendas into structured, fundable, execution-ready models, then build what makes them run: results frameworks, digital platforms, dashboards, quality and governance controls, and the playbooks that let a client sustain the work after we leave.

AHAD Global was established by Shoaib Mohsin, whose two decades leading programme design, monitoring and evaluation, institutional reform and digital transformation for FCDO/UKaid, the World Bank, GIZ, the EU and ETF, and ADB now form the firm's method. We work through a network of associate experts and specialist partners assembled against each mandate: senior attention on every engagement, and a team built for the problem rather than a problem fitted to a standing team.

Leadership

Shoaib Mohsin

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Twenty years designing, measuring and reforming large public and donor-funded programmes: strategy at the top, evidence in the middle, working systems at the delivery end.

  • Programme development and M&E leadership at Pakistan's largest skills development fund, financed by FCDO and the World Bank.
  • M&E architecture for a World Bank-financed human capital investment programme, with a Big 4 professional services firm.
  • Institutional transformation under the EU/ETF vocational excellence framework, securing the first certification of its kind in Pakistan.
  • MS Mechanical Engineering, Bradley University, USA, and MCS Computer Science, University of Karachi.
  • Certified Six Sigma Black Belt and ISO 9001 Lead Auditor, with monitoring and evaluation certification from CERP/J-PAL (MIT) and ITC-ILO Turin.
  • Twenty years across FCDO/UKaid, World Bank, GIZ, EU/ETF and ADB-financed programmes in skills, education, health, social protection and labour mobility.
