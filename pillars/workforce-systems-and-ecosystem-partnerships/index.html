Workforce Systems & Ecosystem Partnerships

Skills systems matched to labour demand, and qualifications that travel across borders.

What we solve

Skills systems that do not match labour demand, and qualifications that do not travel across borders.

Services

  • CoVE development (ISATCOVE)
  • TVET system reform and policy implementation
  • Labour market assessment and occupational profiling
  • EU/German qualification alignment and migration readiness
  • Industry linkages, PPPs and employer-led cost-sharing
  • Gender and inclusion in TVET
  • Digital and financial inclusion, entrepreneurship ecosystems
  • Donor coordination and multi-stakeholder management

Signature deliverables

  • Institutional self-assessment and costed development plan
  • Occupational profiles and sector briefs
  • Qualification alignment and gap statements
  • Employer onboarding kits and cost-sharing models
  • Inclusion strategy and gendered product design

Methods and standards

ISATCOVE self-assessment · NVQF–DQR/EQF alignment · ISCO/ESCO occupational mapping · CBT&A curriculum design · labour market intelligence

Proof anchor

Led the ISATCOVE self-assessment that secured the first ETF Certificate of Commitment to Vocational Excellence issued to a Pakistani institution; NVQF–DQR alignment and ISCO/ESCO mapping under GIZ LABOUR MAP; women's economic empowerment programme reaching 3,000 trainees through 40 partners.

International placement platform

A government-chartered multi-campus vocational and applied skills college
2026–present · Active

Strong training capacity and Gulf and European employer interest, but no operating model to convert either into placements that would withstand employer and regulator scrutiny.

Result

Operating model, screening frameworks and audit trail in build, with the pipeline structured so every placement decision can be reconstructed from evidence.

What was done

Developed the business case securing approval, then designed the operating model: employer onboarding and outreach kits for Gulf and EU entry; candidate screening frameworks covering technical competence, language and employer fit; SOPs, quality assurance and audit checks making each placement decision traceable; pipeline dashboards; and a phased rollout plan with risk register.

Women's economic empowerment flagship — design and RCT

Pakistan's largest skills development fund
2013–2018 · Completed

A flagship women's skills programme required both a fundable design and credible evidence of whether it changed household economics.

Result

11.6% household income increase, four-fold earnings gain for market-linkage graduates, and measurable mobility and civic engagement gains. Findings drove the flagship redesign.

What was done

Authored the concept note and design documents that secured funding, then commissioned a randomised controlled trial and market-linkage pilot across three districts covering 3,000+ female trainees and 40+ delivery partners, tracked over five years.

Urban women's vocational training — tracer and evaluation study

Pakistan's largest skills development fund, with a Big 4 professional services firm
2020 · Completed

A province-wide women's vocational programme needed employability evidence beyond completion rates.

Result

Targeting, industry-linkage and soft-skill gaps identified, and approximately 56% income increase among employed graduates.

What was done

Contributed to a mixed-methods study across nine urban districts and 1,500+ respondents, assessing employability outcomes for 8,000+ trained women through surveys, focus groups and employer consultations.
