Risk & Commercial Controls

Exposure controlled, procurement defensible.

What we solve

Exposure that is known but not controlled, and procurement that cannot be defended.

Services

  • Governance, risk and compliance, including safeguarding
  • Contracts, procurement and vendor performance management

Signature deliverables

  • Risk register and mitigation plan
  • Governance charter
  • Compliance checklists and escalation workflows
  • Procurement plan and vendor scorecards
  • Bid evaluation tools, SLA and KPI clause sets

Methods and standards

GRC frameworks · safeguarding and PSEA protocols · results-based payment verification · vendor performance management

Proof anchor

Compliance controls, risk registers and safeguarding protocols for migration-pathway candidate onboarding; contract compliance and results-based payment systems managed at the national skills fund; procurement and contract management in industrial capital projects.

International placement platform

A government-chartered multi-campus vocational and applied skills college
2026–present · Active

Strong training capacity and Gulf and European employer interest, but no operating model to convert either into placements that would withstand employer and regulator scrutiny.

Result

Operating model, screening frameworks and audit trail in build, with the pipeline structured so every placement decision can be reconstructed from evidence.

What was done

Developed the business case securing approval, then designed the operating model: employer onboarding and outreach kits for Gulf and EU entry; candidate screening frameworks covering technical competence, language and employer fit; SOPs, quality assurance and audit checks making each placement decision traceable; pipeline dashboards; and a phased rollout plan with risk register.

Franchise and replication operating system

A government-chartered multi-campus vocational and applied skills college
2026–present · Active

Expansion demand was outrunning the institute's ability to hold quality constant at distance from the founding campus.

Result

A defined format, a break-even threshold per site, and a control pack that travels with each new campus.

What was done

Structured a repeatable, quality-controlled replication model: format and territory logic; unit economics and break-even modelling; phased rollout architecture; a standardised pack of SOPs, quality-control frameworks and audit checklists; partner onboarding criteria; and brand-protection and escalation controls.

Third-party monitoring and verification system

Pakistan's largest skills development fund, with a Big 4 professional services firm
2014–2018 · Completed

Payments were tied to results across hundreds of delivery partners, with no single assurance framework capable of standing up to audit.

Result

One verification framework covering 500+ partners, with payment decisions traceable to verified delivery evidence.

What was done

Designed and managed the institutional third-party monitoring framework across 500+ delivery partners spanning vocational training, health, education, livestock and gender inclusion: sampling design, verification protocols, delivery-quality and outcome assessment, partner scorecards, and audit-ready evidence trails linked to results-based payment.
