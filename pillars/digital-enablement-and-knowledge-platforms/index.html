Human capital investment programme — M&E architecture

A provincial social protection authority, with a Big 4 professional services firm
2021–2022 · Completed

A multi-sector human capital programme needed one measurement architecture across line departments with different systems, mandates and reporting habits.

Result

A single results architecture and dashboard specification tied to disbursement-linked indicators.

What was done

Designed the integrated M&E framework, logframe indicators and results hierarchies across health, education and service delivery, with information-flow and reporting mechanisms agreed across line departments, and digital dashboards aligned to disbursement-linked indicators. Included gap analysis and risk mitigation strategies.

National-scale skills fund — digital transformation

Pakistan's largest skills development fund
2012–2020 · Completed

The delivery chain — enrolment, verification, stipends, procurement and reporting — ran on manual processes that could not scale or be audited efficiently.

Result

90% improvement in payment processing efficiency, 1M+ records digitised, and a 90% success rate against logframe indicators.

What was done

Led digitisation of the delivery chain: a business support system integrated with national identity and social registry databases through APIs; KPI reporting and executive dashboards; e-procurement; and an MIS with android-based field monitoring. Stipend disbursement moved to 100% mobile payments.
