Governance, Impact Intelligence & Assurance

Measurement that informs decisions rather than satisfying reports.

What we solve

Reporting that satisfies donors but does not inform decisions; verification that cannot withstand audit.

Services

  • MEL system design at project and institution level
  • Third-party monitoring and verification models
  • Impact measurement and performance dashboards

Signature deliverables

  • MEL manual, data collection and flow plans
  • TPM terms of reference, sampling and verification protocols
  • KPI dictionary and indicator hierarchy
  • Executive scorecards and dashboard specifications
  • Evaluation designs — RCT, quasi-experimental, tracer, mixed-methods

Methods and standards

Logframes and results frameworks · intention-to-treat estimation · cost-benefit analysis · sampling and verification protocols · audit-ready evidence trails

Proof anchor

PHCIP integrated M&E framework and digital dashboards under the World Bank; institutional third-party monitoring designed and managed with Ernst & Young across 500+ training partners; 90% success rate against logframe indicators at PSDF.

Rural economy skills programme — impact evaluation

Pakistan's largest skills development fund
2016–2020 · Completed

A rural skills programme needed evidence of whether training was changing production and income, not just attendance.

Result

41% increase in wheat output, 43% in cotton and 17% in milk, with a 5.4-fold first-year return on per-household cost. Findings drove recalibration of training delivery and refresher mechanisms.

What was done

Directed an experimental evaluation across 90 villages (60 treatment, 30 control) in four high-poverty districts: baseline and endline household surveys, intention-to-treat estimation and full cost-benefit analysis.

Women's economic empowerment flagship — design and RCT

Pakistan's largest skills development fund
2013–2018 · Completed

A flagship women's skills programme required both a fundable design and credible evidence of whether it changed household economics.

Result

11.6% household income increase, four-fold earnings gain for market-linkage graduates, and measurable mobility and civic engagement gains. Findings drove the flagship redesign.

What was done

Authored the concept note and design documents that secured funding, then commissioned a randomised controlled trial and market-linkage pilot across three districts covering 3,000+ female trainees and 40+ delivery partners, tracked over five years.

Urban women's vocational training — tracer and evaluation study

Pakistan's largest skills development fund, with a Big 4 professional services firm
2020 · Completed

A province-wide women's vocational programme needed employability evidence beyond completion rates.

Result

Targeting, industry-linkage and soft-skill gaps identified, and approximately 56% income increase among employed graduates.

What was done

Contributed to a mixed-methods study across nine urban districts and 1,500+ respondents, assessing employability outcomes for 8,000+ trained women through surveys, focus groups and employer consultations.
