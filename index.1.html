The method

Frame. Structure. Enable.

Most organisations do not need another strategy document. They need the distance between a good idea and a working system closed, in the right order.

01

Frame

We test the thing itself before commitments are made. Is the demand real, is the evidence there, where does this fail, and what would have to be true for it to work.

02

Structure

The framed opportunity becomes a fundable, executable model: strategy and portfolio architecture, programme and bid design, theories of change, logframes, unit economics, governance and risk.

03

Enable

We build what makes it run: digital platforms, knowledge systems, performance dashboards, quality controls, and the playbooks and SOPs the client owns outright.

International placement platform

A government-chartered multi-campus vocational and applied skills college
2026–present · Active

Strong training capacity and Gulf and European employer interest, but no operating model to convert either into placements that would withstand employer and regulator scrutiny.

Result

Operating model, screening frameworks and audit trail in build, with the pipeline structured so every placement decision can be reconstructed from evidence.

What was done

Developed the business case securing approval, then designed the operating model: employer onboarding and outreach kits for Gulf and EU entry; candidate screening frameworks covering technical competence, language and employer fit; SOPs, quality assurance and audit checks making each placement decision traceable; pipeline dashboards; and a phased rollout plan with risk register.

Women's economic empowerment flagship — design and RCT

Pakistan's largest skills development fund
2013–2018 · Completed

A flagship women's skills programme required both a fundable design and credible evidence of whether it changed household economics.

Result

11.6% household income increase, four-fold earnings gain for market-linkage graduates, and measurable mobility and civic engagement gains. Findings drove the flagship redesign.

What was done

Authored the concept note and design documents that secured funding, then commissioned a randomised controlled trial and market-linkage pilot across three districts covering 3,000+ female trainees and 40+ delivery partners, tracked over five years.

National-scale skills fund — digital transformation

Pakistan's largest skills development fund
2012–2020 · Completed

The delivery chain — enrolment, verification, stipends, procurement and reporting — ran on manual processes that could not scale or be audited efficiently.

Result

90% improvement in payment processing efficiency, 1M+ records digitised, and a 90% success rate against logframe indicators.

What was done

Led digitisation of the delivery chain: a business support system integrated with national identity and social registry databases through APIs; KPI reporting and executive dashboards; e-procurement; and an MIS with android-based field monitoring. Stipend disbursement moved to 100% mobile payments.

Centre of vocational excellence — institutional transformation

A government-chartered multi-campus vocational and applied skills college
2024–present · Active

The institute intended to benchmark itself against a European excellence framework without a baseline, an evidence base or a costed improvement plan.

Result

The first certification of its kind awarded to an institution in the country, with a costed development plan the institution now works to.

What was done

Led institutional self-assessment across three excellence dimensions and 23 criteria areas covering governance, teaching and learning, partnerships, labour-market responsiveness and digital and green transition, with evidence collection, benchmarking and a costed institutional development plan.
