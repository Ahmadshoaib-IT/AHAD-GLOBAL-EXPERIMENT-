How we engage

Four modes, matched to the size of the problem.

Fees are quoted against written scope and stated assumptions. If an assumption fails, we re-scope in writing before the cost changes.

1–4 weeks · fixed fee

Rapid Sprint

A discrete design or bid deliverable, scoped and priced before it starts.

4–12 weeks · monthly

Strategic Advisory Retainer

Sustained structuring support across a live mandate, on an agreed cadence.

6–16+ weeks · retainer plus milestones

Embedded Delivery Partner

We work inside your team on the build, and hand over documentation designed to run without us.

12–36 months · by exception

Strategic Partner (equity)

Co-owned growth engines where we carry design and delivery risk alongside the platform owner.

For consulting firms, primes and institutes

We work to your client relationships, not around them.

We sign a non-solicitation undertaking covering your named accounts and personnel before proposal work begins. Your client stays your client.

We join bids as a named technical partner, sub-consultant or consortium member, contributing pillar capability and key expert credentials to your technical submission.

  • Non-solicitation undertaking signed before proposal work begins.
  • Key expert CVs tailored to your ToR and returned within 48 hours.
  • Evaluation-grid responses written against the actual scoring criteria.
  • Compliance matrices, methodology sections, workplans and results frameworks.
  • Named-expert availability confirmed before submission, not after award.

Procurement & compliance

The dossier your procurement team will ask for.

Issued as a single pack on request, under NDA where the contracting authority requires it.

Legal status
Certificate of Incorporation, SECP Pakistan. Memorandum and Articles of Association available on request.
Tax registration
National Tax Number and sales tax registration details issued with the dossier.
Capability statement
Firm profile, pillar capability summary and reference project list, formatted for technical submissions.
Key expert CVs
Founder and associate expert CVs, tailored to a specific terms of reference and returned within 48 hours.
Policy set
Safeguarding and PSEA · Anti-corruption and anti-bribery · Data protection · Responsible use of AI · Equal opportunity.
Non-solicitation undertaking
Signed undertaking covering your named accounts and personnel for the engagement and an agreed period after.
Banking and insurance
Bank details, and professional indemnity arrangements where required by the contracting authority.
References
Named referees released with permission, against a specific bid or mandate.

Documents are issued to a named procurement contact rather than published for download, so that every copy in circulation is current.

Delivery partners

How we resource a mandate.

We assemble against the problem: associate experts for evaluation, curriculum and qualification work; specialist development firms for mid-scale technology builds; and enterprise technology partners where deployment is compliance-heavy. Partner names are published only where a co-branding agreement is in place.

Currently open to conversation with: consulting firms and primes bidding in TVET, labour mobility, social protection and public sector reform; institutes planning expansion or excellence certification; and enterprises building a digital or franchise growth engine.

What we ask for in return

  • A clear scope and a named counterpart.
  • Access to the data the analysis requires.
  • Accurate attribution in submissions and published material.
